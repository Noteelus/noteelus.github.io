Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Developer and contact

Noteelus is an independent software project. Contact: open an issue at github.com/Noteelus/noteelus.github.io/issues or write to noteelus-support@googlegroups.com.

Local storage and zero data collection

Notes and attachments are stored on the user's own device, in the app's private storage. There is no Noteelus account and no Noteelus server. The developer receives no data: no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no telemetry. Crash logs stay on the device.

Optional Google Drive sync

If the user chooses to connect their Google account (or multiple Google accounts across Spaces), the app uses Google sign-in (OAuth) with the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file only. That scope lets the app see and change only the files and folders it created or opened itself, never the rest of the user's Google Drive.

The app creates a "Noteelus" folder in the user's own Google Drive and keeps the notes there as files. Data travels directly between the user's device and Google; it never goes through a server of the developer. Sign-in tokens stay locally on the device.

The user can disconnect in the app at any time and can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app deletes its local data; files already in the user's Drive stay there until the user deletes them.

Noteelus's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Encrypted containers

Encrypted containers seal note titles, bodies, folders and attached files client-side with authenticated encryption before storage and before optional upload to Google Drive. Neither Google nor the developer holds your container passwords or recovery keys.

Optional online lookups inside notes

Offline map rendering uses embedded Natural Earth coastlines and places bundled inside the app. Only if a user explicitly runs an online place search inside a map block or adds a user-configured ::api block does the app contact the requested endpoint directly from the device.

Export and share

Export and share (a note as .md, .png or .pdf, a folder or Space as a .zip, text through the system share sheet) happen only when the user asks, to the destination the user chooses.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may change, and updates are posted on this site with a revised date.
