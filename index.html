Is Noteelus free?

Yes. Noteelus is completely free, with no subscription, no ads, and no account required.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Your notes, photos, budgets, and travel maps all work without an internet connection.

Can I sync between my computer and phone?

Yes. If you choose to connect Google Drive, your notes sync directly through your own Drive folder.

What if I forget a locked folder's password?

You can unlock it with the recovery key shown when you create the lock. Nobody else has access to your private folders.
